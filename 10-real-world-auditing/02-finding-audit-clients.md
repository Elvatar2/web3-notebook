# Finding Your First Audit Clients

## Simple Definition
Getting your first paid audit clients requires proving your competence before you have a track record. This is done by building a public portfolio, participating in competitive audits, and strategic networking.

## The Best Analogy
Think of finding audit clients like a **chef trying to open a new restaurant**. No one will pay $100 for a meal from an unknown chef. So, you first cook for free at community events (CTFs), win cooking competitions (Code4rena), and post your recipes online (Twitter/GitHub). Once people taste your skill, they will happily pay for a private dinner (solo audit).

## Step-by-Step Strategy to Get Clients

```text
Phase 1: Build Proof of Work (Months 1-3)
- Solve 15+ Ethernaut/Damn Vulnerable DeFi challenges.
- Write high-quality, public writeups for every solution.
- Publish these on GitHub and Medium/Dev.to.

Phase 2: Competitive Auditing (Months 3-6)
- Participate in Code4rena or Sherlock contests.
- Even if you don't win top prizes, submit valid findings.
- Your name on a public leaderboard is your resume.

Phase 3: Public Networking (Months 6-9)
- Be active in Web3 security Discords (Immunefi, Code4rena).
- Reply to protocol announcements with thoughtful security questions.
- Post "mini-audits" of new, small protocols on Twitter for free.

Phase 4: Cold Outreach (Months 9+)
- Identify small-to-medium DeFi protocols that just raised a seed round.
- Send a professional DM or email to the founder/CTO.
- Offer a discounted initial audit or a free "pre-audit review" to build trust.
```

## Cold Outreach Email Template

```text
Subject: Security Review for [Protocol Name] - [Your Name]

Hi [Founder/CTO Name],

I've been following [Protocol Name] and love the approach to [specific feature].

I'm a smart contract security researcher. I recently placed in the top 10% of a Code4rena audit for [Similar Protocol] and have published detailed writeups on my GitHub: [Link].

I noticed you're preparing for a mainnet launch. I'd love to offer a complimentary, high-level security review of your core contracts to help identify any obvious vulnerabilities before you engage a formal audit firm.

No strings attached. Let me know if you're open to a brief 15-minute chat.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Link to your portfolio/Twitter]
```

## Key Takeaways
- **Reputation is Currency:** In Web3 security, your public track record is your most valuable asset.
- **Start Small:** Don't pitch to Aave or Uniswap on day one. Target new, smaller projects.
- **Give Value First:** Offering a free, high-level review builds immense trust and often leads to paid work.
- **Professionalism Matters:** Communicate clearly, meet deadlines, and be easy to work with. Technical skill gets you in the door; professionalism keeps you there.
- **Leverage Communities:** Many solo auditors get their first clients through referrals from other auditors who are fully booked.
